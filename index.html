Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and other UPI apps). After you place your order, we confirm the total including delivery on WhatsApp and share our UPI details.

Do you deliver to my town?

Yes! We deliver to your home anywhere in India by courier. Delivery charges depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

How long does a custom order take?

Usually 3–7 days depending on design and quantity. For bulk orders please message us early.

What are the candles made of?

Our candles are handmade with pure soya wax and lavender fragrance. Terracotta diyas use natural clay.

Safety tips

Burn candles on a heat-safe surface, away from children, pets, curtains and anything that can catch fire. Never leave a burning candle unattended.
