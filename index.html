Questions

Frequently asked

How do I pay?

After you send your order on WhatsApp, we confirm the total and share UPI details. Cash on delivery may be available for nearby locations.

Do you deliver to my town?

Yes, we deliver across India. Delivery charges depend on your location and are confirmed on WhatsApp before payment.

How long does a custom order take?

Usually 3–7 days depending on design and quantity. For bulk orders please message us early.

How long does a candle burn?

It depends on size — the burn time is mentioned with each product. Trim the wick and burn for 2–3 hours the first time for best results.

Is it safe?

Always burn candles on a heat-safe surface, away from children, pets, curtains and anything that can catch fire. Never leave a burning candle unattended.
